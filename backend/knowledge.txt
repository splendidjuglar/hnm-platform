# HNM Food Groups Knowledge Base

## Company Overview

Company Name: HNM Food Groups

HNM Food Groups is a Wales-based food company focused on creating clean-label breakfast cereals using naturally sourced ingredients. The company aims to provide nutritious, high-quality cereal products that meet modern nutritional standards while remaining free from unnecessary artificial additives and preservatives.

Headquarters:
14 Museum Place, Cardiff, Wales, CF10 3BH

Mission:
To provide wholesome, naturally sourced breakfast products that support healthier lifestyles for families and individuals.

Core Values:

* Natural Ingredients
* Clean Label Formulations
* Quality and Transparency
* Nutritional Excellence
* Family-Focused Nutrition

## Products

### HNM Multi Grain Cereal

Status:
Product in Development

Expected Launch:
Q4 2026

Description:
HNM Multi Grain Cereal is a premium breakfast cereal crafted from carefully selected multi grain ingredients. The product is designed to provide balanced nutrition while maintaining a clean-label formulation.

Key Features:

* Naturally sourced ingredients
* No artificial additives
* No artificial flavourings
* No synthetic preservatives
* No added sugar
* Plant-based protein source
* Rich in essential vitamins
* Contains iron and B vitamins
* Suitable for adults and children over 12 months

Ingredient Philosophy:
HNM Food Groups prioritises naturally sourced ingredients and aims to work with high-quality agricultural suppliers. Organic sourcing and certification processes are being explored and remain subject to certification approval.
